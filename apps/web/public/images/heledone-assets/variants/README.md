# Retained Coastal Variants

These original ImageGen outputs are kept in the project even when they are not currently used by the interface. Do not remove them as unused assets. The final panoramas remain in the parent directory, including the unused new dashboard panorama; the Home screen uses its previous artwork again.

## Files

- `dashboard-coastal-half-space.png`: original dashboard and personal workspace composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `organizations-coastal-half-space.png`: original organizations and organization details composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `teams-coastal-half-space.png`: original teams composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `users-coastal-half-space.png`: original people composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `roles-coastal-half-space.png`: original roles and permissions composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `profile-coastal-half-space.png`: original personal profile composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `settings-coastal-half-space.png`: original settings composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `attendance-coastal-half-space.png`: original attendance and time composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `finance-coastal-half-space.png`: original finance and reports composition, with about half the canvas left quiet.
- `projects-coastal-half-space.png`: original Projects illustration before the two-thirds composition edit.

## Original Prompts

### dashboard-coastal-half-space.png

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset: new Heledone Dashboard and personal workspace header bitmap.
Scene: A bright Bushehr waterfront greeting: a low whitewashed house with turquoise shenashir balcony, coral bougainvillea, a curved stone quay and a small wooden lenj on sparkling water, one palm casting delicate shade. Fresh morning daylight.
Style: polished painterly gouache and watercolor, mature charming editorial illustration, richly crafted recognizable southern Iranian details, tactile brushwork, crisp clear subjects, no vector style.
Composition: 3:1 wide panorama. Confine the entire scene and its main subjects to the LEFT 44% of the canvas, readable in a short horizontal middle crop. RIGHT 55% is completely clean near-white #FFFCF8 negative space for bilingual interface text; only an extremely faint wash at the boundary. No horizon lines or objects in the right half.
Palette: fresh turquoise and teal #087F83, coral #DF765B, small sun-yellow #F2BA49 accents, palm green, restrained natural wood, mostly near-white. Never predominantly brown, beige or orange.
Constraints: brand-new illustration, opaque background, no people, text, lettering, logos, watermark, UI, frame, vignette or floating decorative shapes.
```

### projects-coastal-half-space.png

```text
Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: a new production bitmap header panorama for Heledone's bilingual Projects workspace.
Primary request: a fresh, inviting southern-Iran coastal illustration with the warmth of Bushehr and the feeling of building something together.
Scene and subject: a traditional wooden Persian Gulf lenj moored at a small working harbor on the LEFT, with beautiful visible hull craftsmanship, a tidy waterfront boat workshop with teal shenashir wooden details, one palm, a few coral bougainvillea blossoms, clear turquoise water and soft sunny light. No people.
Style: polished hand-painted gouache and watercolor editorial illustration, crisp recognizable southern Iranian details, tactile brushwork, charming but mature, not photographic, not a vector.
Composition: very wide 3:1 panorama. All architecture, palm and the complete main boat confined to the left 42% of the canvas. Keep the boat readable in a short horizontal middle crop. The right 55% must be clean near-white (#FFFCF8) negative space for UI heading and buttons; allow only a very faint wash at the far-left boundary of that quiet area. No objects, horizon lines, waves or shadows in the rightmost half. Bright neutral backdrop, no vignette.
Palette: turquoise and deep teal #087F83, coral #DF765B, small sun-yellow #F2BA49 accents, palm green, natural warm wood and mostly near-white canvas. Avoid a brown or beige-dominated image.
Constraints: brand-new scene, no typography, no lettering on the boat, no logos, no watermark, no UI elements, no frame, no decorative floating shapes. Opaque background.
```

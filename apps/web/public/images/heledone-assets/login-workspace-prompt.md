Primary request: create one beautiful, welcoming, editorial hand-painted illustration that connects focused team work with the Persian Gulf, not a travel poster.

Style: sophisticated gouache and watercolor with tactile brushwork, clear recognizable objects, bright natural light, inviting rather than corporate, carefully composed rather than scattered stickers. Composition: wide 16:9 wallpaper. Concentrate the detailed desk and window in the outer LEFT 28 percent, and the small shoreline in the outer RIGHT 22 percent. The two illustrated edges should have organic clean contours fading via sparse paint texture into transparency, NOT a rectangular picture frame. The desk rests at the bottom edge and the window is fully visible. No object, wave, shadow, or palm leaf may cross the central form space. The top-center is empty too.
Constraints: real transparent background everywhere outside the artwork; no text, lettering, logos, watermark, people, UI mockup, fake buttons, icons, gradients, abstract blobs, or glowing orbs.
